About me
======
I'm a PhD student at [Computer Vision Group, Technical University of Munich](https://cvg.cit.tum.de/members/wxu), co-supervised by [Prof. Dr. Daniel Cremers](https://cvg.cit.tum.de/members/cremers) and [Prof. Dr.-Ing. Niclas Zeller](https://cvg.cit.tum.de/members/zlniclas).

My research is focused on **Generative AI**, **Optimal Transport**, and **Agentic AI**, with applications in 3D Computer Vision including novel view synthesis, domain translation, and scene understanding.

I obtained my Diplome d'ingenieur (M.S.) from [Telecom Paris](https://www.telecom-paris.fr/) in 2022, following the completion of my B.S. from [Shanghai Jiao Tong University](https://en.sjtu.edu.cn/) in 2020.
